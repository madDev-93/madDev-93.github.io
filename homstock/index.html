Frequently asked

How does receipt scanning work?

Open HomStock, tap the scan button, and point your camera at a grocery receipt. The app reads the line items, sorts each product into a category (Fridge, Pantry, Frozen, etc.), and adds them to your stock automatically. You can edit or delete anything before saving.

Where is my data stored?

You sign in with Apple, and your inventory, receipts, and shopping lists are stored securely in Google Firebase. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and only members of your household can see it. We never sell or share your data with advertisers. See the Privacy Policy for full details.

How does household sharing work?

Invite the people in your home to join your household, and everyone sees the same shared inventory, shopping lists, and receipts in real time. No more buying duplicates because someone else already grabbed it.

A receipt didn't scan correctly. What can I do?

Make sure the receipt is well-lit, flat, and fully in frame. If specific line items are misread, you can tap any item after a scan to edit the name, category, or price. If you keep running into issues with a particular store's receipts, email us a sample and we'll improve recognition for it.

How do I delete an item or a whole scan?

Swipe left on any item in your Stock or Shopping List to delete it. To delete an entire scanned receipt, open the receipt from your scan history and tap Delete.

Can I use HomStock on iPad?

HomStock is built for iPhone. It will run on iPad in compatibility mode, but the experience is optimized for phone-sized screens.

Do I need an internet connection?

Yes. HomStock signs you in with Apple and syncs your household data through Firebase, so an internet connection is required for receipt scanning, syncing, and sharing with household members.
