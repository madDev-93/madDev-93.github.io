HomStock

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Overview

HomStock ("the App") is a household inventory management app developed by Jorge Mendoza. This policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

The short version: Your data is stored securely in Firebase and is only shared with members of your household. We do not sell your data.

2. Data We Collect

When you use HomStock, the following data is collected and stored:

  • Account information: Your Apple ID display name and email (via Sign in with Apple)
  • Household data: Product inventory, shopping lists, and household member information
  • Receipt data: Photos of receipts, extracted item names, prices, and store information
  • Device tokens: For push notifications when household members share updates
  • Subscription status: Whether you have an active HomStock Pro subscription, the product purchased, transaction identifiers, and expiration dates (used to grant Pro features and for subscription analytics)

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide household inventory tracking and shopping list features
  • To parse receipt photos using Google Gemini AI for item extraction
  • To send push notifications to household members
  • To calculate spending breakdowns and product price history

4. Data Storage & Security

All data is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore and Cloud Storage), secured with Firebase Authentication and Firestore security rules. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest.

Receipt images are stored in Firebase Cloud Storage and are accessible only to authenticated household members.

5. AI Processing

Receipt photos are processed using Google Gemini AI via Firebase Cloud Functions. Only extracted OCR text is sent to Google's Gemini API for parsing — images are not sent to AI. Google's AI services are subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

6. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

HomStock Pro is offered as a monthly or annual auto-renewable subscription. Payment is processed by Apple via the App Store; we never see your payment information.

To verify entitlement and provide subscription analytics, we share the following data with RevenueCat, Inc. (a third-party subscription infrastructure provider):

  • A pseudonymous user identifier (your Firebase UID)
  • Apple-issued transaction metadata: product identifier, purchase date, expiration date, original transaction ID, and environment (Sandbox or Production)
  • Country code and currency of the transaction

RevenueCat does not receive your name, email, receipt photos, or any other app content. RevenueCat's processing is governed by its Privacy Policy and Data Processing Addendum.

7. Data Sharing

Your data is shared only with:

  • Your household members: Other users you invite can see shared inventory, shopping lists, and receipts
  • Google Firebase: For authentication, data storage, cloud functions, and remote configuration
  • Google Gemini AI: Receipt text for parsing only
  • Apple: Payment processing for in-app subscriptions
  • RevenueCat: Subscription entitlement verification and analytics (see section 6)

We do not sell, rent, or trade your data with advertisers or data brokers.

8. Data Deletion

  • You can delete individual receipts, products, and shopping list items within the App
  • You can leave a household to remove your association with shared data
  • To request complete account deletion, contact us at the email below

9. Children's Privacy

HomStock is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
