2. The short version

HomStock stores your household data in Google Firebase. It is shared only with members of your household and the limited service providers listed below. We do not sell or rent your data, we don't run ads, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

6. International data transfers

Your data is stored on servers in the United States (Google Cloud, us-central1). Where these transfers fall under the GDPR or UK GDPR, they are covered by the Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) incorporated into the Firebase Data Processing and Security Terms and Google's broader Cloud Data Processing Addendum, and supplemented by Google's technical and organizational safeguards described in those documents.

7. How long we keep your data

  • Account, household, receipts, shopping items, products: retained for as long as your account exists.
  • Push notification tokens: automatically removed when a device no longer accepts deliveries.
  • App preferences: stored on your device and removed when you delete the App.
  • Subscription transaction records: retained for as long as required to provide service and meet legal/accounting obligations.
  • Backups: deleted data may persist in Google Cloud's encrypted backups for up to 180 days before being purged, as described in Google's Data Processing and Security Terms.

8. What happens when you delete your account

You can delete your account at any time from Account → Delete Account in the App. When you do:

  • Your user profile, Firebase Auth record, and subscription ledger entries are permanently deleted.
  • If you are the sole member of a household, the entire household is deleted: its receipts, shopping list, product catalog, and invite code are removed.
  • If you are a member of a household that still has other members, you are removed from the member list. Receipts you scanned, shopping items you added, and products you created remain visible to the remaining household members, because that content is also their household record. The App tells you this before you confirm deletion.
  • If you are the household owner and other members remain, ownership transfers to the longest-tenured remaining member.

To revoke Sign in with Apple after deletion, go to iOS Settings → your Apple ID → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple → HomStock → Stop Using Apple ID.

9. Your rights under GDPR and UK GDPR

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access (Article 15) — get a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification (Article 16) — correct inaccurate or incomplete data. Most data can be edited directly in the App.
  • Erasure (Article 17) — delete your account using Account → Delete Account, or email us.
  • Portability (Article 20) — download a machine-readable copy of your data using Account → Privacy & data → Export My Data in the App. The export is provided as a JSON file.
  • Restriction (Article 18) — ask us to pause processing of certain data while a dispute is resolved.
  • Objection (Article 21) — object to processing we carry out under "legitimate interests".
  • Withdraw consent — where we ever ask for consent, you can withdraw it at any time.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority. A list of EU/EEA authorities is available at edpb.europa.eu/about-edpb/about-edpb/members. For the UK, the ICO is at ico.org.uk.

To exercise any of these rights, email jomen12@icloud.com. We will respond within 30 days as required by Article 12.

10. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest by Google Cloud. Access is gated by Firebase Authentication and Firestore security rules so only authenticated members of your household can read your data. App Check is enabled to protect Cloud Functions against abuse. We do not have direct access to your receipt photos or shopping lists; we only see them in aggregate when troubleshooting at your request.

If we ever discover a personal-data breach that is likely to result in risk to you, we will notify the relevant supervisory authority within 72 hours and contact you directly when required.

11. Children

HomStock is not directed to, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from, anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
